RUNNING ON EMPTY
A free reader's guide
By Dr. Sandra Cortina, MD

Running on Empty offers a framework for understanding obesity through biology, with clarity instead of self-blame. Use these prompts to reflect as you read, or bring a question to a conversation with a healthcare professional.

PAUSE AND REFLECT

1. What messages have you heard about willpower, hunger, or weight? Which of those messages would you like to examine more closely?

2. How might understanding biological factors change the way you interpret a difficult or confusing experience?

3. What questions about obesity, treatment, or GLP-1 medications would you like to discuss with a healthcare professional?

4. What would a compassionate next step look like for you, without treating weight as a finish line?

This guide is for personal reflection and general education. It is not medical advice and does not replace care from a qualified healthcare professional.
