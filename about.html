Her work

Evidence over myths.
Care over blame.

Dr. Cortina is passionate about helping patients unlearn diet culture myths and replace them with evidence-based approaches to clinically meaningful weight loss.

She practices obesity medicine in the heart of New Westminster, British Columbia, and virtually through an online clinic open to patients across the province. She has a special interest in ADHD and Binge Eating Disorder, and in managing the many causes of obesity that help make treatment succeed over the long term.

As an advocate for science communication, she shares accurate weight-management information on social media and works to debunk misleading trends. Running on Empty carries that same mission: a clear, compassionate explanation of the biology that diet culture leaves out.
